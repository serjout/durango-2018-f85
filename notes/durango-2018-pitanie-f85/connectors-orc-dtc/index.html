    Connector-level wiring of the ORC power feeds (F85/F86 → PDC C6 → ORC) — 2018 Dodge Durango (WD) 5.7L AWD¶

    Локальные копии документов

    Пути вида docs/sources/... указывают на копии документов в приватном репозитории, на сайте их нет. Пользуйтесь ссылками на оригинальные страницы или указателем источников.

    Source basis: everything marked VERIFIED was read from the 2018 Durango factory service information, as reproduced on lemon-manuals.la. The primary manual used is "2018 Durango Citadel, 5.7L Eng VIN T, AWD". The key pages were also pulled from the 2018 "Durango R/T, AWD" manual: the C6 table, ORC C2 table and B212C/B212D test text are byte-identical, and the wiring diagrams use the same image IDs (VA582314, VA586498–501). So the data applies to the subject R/T AWD, VIN 1C4SDJCT3JCXXXXXX. Wiring diagrams are SVG files and were rendered to PNG locally for reading. Pin/colour data come from the HTML tables, not from OCR.

    Wire colour codes: 2017–2018 SI uses ISO-style codes (PK = pink, YE = yellow, GN = green, BK = black, BU = blue, DB = dark blue). The diagrams spell them out (PNK/YEL, PNK/GRN, BLK). The 2015 SI uses old Chrysler codes (YL, LG = light green) and AWG gauges.

    Q1. Which PDC connector is C6? Is it D6630F? What is the 2018 pinout, and does it match the reported 2015 data?¶

    Takeaway¶

    VERIFIED. PDC C6 is harness connector D6630F for 2018: LIGHT GRAY, FEMALE, Body harness, part number 7287-8882-40. The 2015 data mostly holds: pin 15 = F929, pin 13 = F945, pin 28 = F931, pin 32 = F925, pin 36 = F948. Only the colour notation changed: 2015 PK/YL and PK/LG are 2018 PK/YE and PK/GN.

    Cited Findings¶

    • VERIFIED: "Assembly-Power Distribution Center C6 (Standard) Connector End View (D6630F)". Harness-Family Body, Color LIGHT GRAY, Gender FEMALE, Part Number 7287-8882-40, Connector No D6630F. — 2018 PDC C6 end view
    • VERIFIED: 2018 C6 (D6630F) pinout, complete list as published ("Pins not listed are not used"). Columns are pin – circuit – colour – mm² – function. — 2018 PDC C6 end view
    • 1 A400 BG/RD 2.0 FUSED B(+); 2 A949 RD 2.0 FUSED B(+); 3 C15 DB/WH 2.0 REAR WINDOW DEFOGGER CONTROL OUTPUT; 4 C115 DB 0.50 DEFOGGER RELAY CONTROL
    • 7 F949 PK/GN 0.50 FUSED IGNITION RUN/START CONTROL OUTPUT; 8 F942 PK/GN 0.35 FUSED IGNITION SWITCH OUTPUT (RUN-START); 10 K617 BN 0.50 RUN/START RELAY CONTROL
    • 13 F945 PK/GN 0.50 IGNITION RUN/START CONTROL OUTPUT
    • 15 F929 PK/YE 0.50 FUSED IGNITION SWITCH OUTPUT (RUN)
    • 16 L310 BG/WH 0.50 RIGHT HEADLAMP RELAY CONTROL; 17 A130 VT/RD 2.0 FUSED B(+); 19 A918 RD 2.0 FUSED B(+); 21 A904 RD 4.0 FUSED B(+); 24 A111 DG/RD 2.0 FUSED B(+); 26 A932 RD 1.0 FUSED B(+)
    • 28 F931 PK/YE 0.50 FUSED IGNITION RUN CONTROL OUTPUT; 32 F925 PK/YE 0.50 FUSED IGNITION RUN CONTROL OUTPUT; 34 F938 PK/YE 0.50 FUSED IGNITION RUN/START RELAY OUTPUT; 36 F948 PK/GN 0.35 FUSED IGNITION RUN/START CONTROL OUTPUT
    • 38 A401 RD/GN 2.0 FUSED B(+); 39 K90 BU/BG 0.50 STARTER RELAY CONTROL
    • VERIFIED: the 2018 R/T AWD manual's C6 page has an identical pin table and the same part number and colour. — 2018 R/T AWD PDC C6
    • VERIFIED (end-view figure, image GCWD1006494): the cavity numbering callouts are 1 (upper right), 21 (upper left), 22 (lower right) and 42 (lower left). Pins 4 and 5 are called out as an inner small terminal and an outer slot. So numbering alternates between staggered sub-rows; this is a 42-way layout. — C6 end-view image
    • VERIFIED: the PDC layout graphic ("DODGE DURANGO (WD) Underhood Power Distribution Center (PDC)") shows C6 plugged into the rear/top-left area of the PDC. Its fuse table lists F85 "Airbag Module" 10A, F86 "Airbag Module" 10A and F95 "Rear Camera / ParkSense" 10A. — PDC Layout And Connector Identification (image G00621551)
    • STRONG INFERENCE (other year, same connector): the 2015 R/T AWD C6 page (D6630F, LIGHT GRAY, same PN 7287-8882-40) lists 13 F945 PK/LG 22 ga, 15 F929 PK/YL 22 ga, 28 F931 PK/YL, 32 F925 PK/YL, 36 F948 PK/LG. This confirms the user's 2015 data. — 2015 R/T AWD PDC C6
    • STRONG INFERENCE: 2017 R/T AWD C6 is identical to 2018 (PK/YE, PK/GN, metric sizes). — 2017 R/T AWD PDC C6
    • VERIFIED (what the other cited pins feed, from Power Distribution diagram 1 of 4, VA586498):
    • C6-28 (F931) is the load side of fuse 103 10A. It is jumpered to C7-8, which goes to the Rear Blower Motor Relay coil (85).
    • C6-32 (F925) is the load side of fuse 101 15A. Path: XY5020A-7 → S2443 → in-car temperature sensor and humidity sensor.
    • C6-34 is the load side of fuse 100 10A. Path: XY6020A-33 → XY8200A-8 → SP2589 → Active Damping Control Module C2-14/47.
    • C6-36 (F948) is the load side of fuse 99 10A on the Run/Start relay (diagram 3 of 4, VA586500). Path: S2448 → Park Assist, A/C heater module C1-1, Forward Facing Camera, Rear A/C heater control module, Adaptive Cruise.
    • Sources: Power Distribution diagrams page; diagrams VA586498 and VA586500

    Inferences¶

    • The connector identification needs no correction for 2018: C6 = D6630F, same part number since 2015, same circuit codes. Only the colour notation changed (PK/LG → PK/GN; PK/YL → PK/YE) and gauges moved from AWG to mm² (22 ga ≈ 0.35–0.5 mm²).
    • In the C6 table only F929 (pin 15) is described as "FUSED IGNITION SWITCH OUTPUT (RUN)". Pins 28 and 32 are "FUSED IGNITION RUN CONTROL OUTPUT". All three PK/YE circuits come off the same Run 2 relay output bus (see Q2).

    Gaps¶

    • The exact physical cavity location of pin 15 within the staggered 42-way face was not resolved. The figure only labels pins 1, 4, 5, 21, 22 and 42; use the numbers moulded on the connector. The table also does not state whether the end view shows the terminal side or the wire side.
    • The PDC fuse/relay table graphic is low-resolution (853×635 px). The fuse numbers are legible; the relay "Alternate" codes are not reliably legible.

    Q2. Is C6 pin 15 the output (load side) of F85 or an input? What feeds F85?¶

    Takeaway¶

    VERIFIED. C6-15 is the load (output) side of fuse 85. Inside the PDC, F85's supply side is fed by the RUN 2 relay (contact 87). F85 output → C6-15 → PK/YE F929 → ORC C2-104. The same Run 2 relay output also feeds fuses 40, 103, 101 and 100. The Run 2 relay coil (85) is fed from the Run 1 relay output bus, which also feeds F95. The coil return (86) shares the PDC ground bus with the Run 1 relay coil: PDC C1-12 → S2547 → G909A.

    Cited Findings¶

    • VERIFIED (SRS diagram 1 of 3, VA582314): the PDC block shows "FUSE 85 10A" labelled "HOT W/ RUN 2 RELAY ENERGIZED" and "FUSE 86 10A" labelled "HOT W/ RUN/START RELAY ENERGIZED". Fuse 85's lower terminal exits at C6 pin 15 → PNK/YEL → ORC pin 104 "FUSED IGN SW OUTPUT (RUN)" F929. Fuse 86 exits at C6 pin 13 → PNK/GRN → ORC pin 103 "IGN RUN/START CONTROL OUTPUT" F945. — Supplemental Restraints diagrams, image VA582314
    • VERIFIED (Power Distribution diagram 1 of 4, VA586498): RUN 2 RELAY contact 30 connects to the PDC B+ bus. Contact 87 drives a bus feeding FUSE 40 10A, FUSE 103 10A, FUSE 85 10A, FUSE 101 15A and FUSE 100 10A.
    • Fuse 85 → pin 15 → PNK/YEL → "104 C2 OCCUPANT RESTRAINT CONTROLLER MODULE". The dashed connector line joins pins 15, 32 and 34, and the group is labelled C6.
    • No in-line connector or splice is drawn on this run.
    • Source: Power Distribution diagrams, image VA586498; local crop renders/2018_Durango_PD1of4_crop_RUN2_relay_F85_C6pin15.png
    • VERIFIED (same diagram): RUN 1 RELAY contact 87 drives fuses 95 (10A), 96 (10A), 98 (20A) and 97 (20A). The same Run 1 output line also connects to the RUN 2 RELAY coil terminal 85 and the (front) BLOWER MOTOR RELAY coil terminal 85.
    • Run 1 relay coil 85 → C7-12 → BRN/ORG → Body Control Module C5-36.
    • Run 1 and Run 2 relay coil terminals 86 join a common line inside the PDC.
    • Source: VA586498; local crop renders/2018_Durango_PD1of4_crop_RUN1_RUN2_relay_coils.png
    • VERIFIED (Ground Distribution diagram 1 of 4, VA586554): inside the PDC, coil terminals of several relays tie to one bus that exits PDC C1 pin 12 → BLK → S2547 (front fascia, front end module harness) → XY8120A-6 → G909A (right rear of engine compartment). The relays are Run 1 (86), Run 2 (86), Run/Accessory 1 and 2, Run/Start (86), Horn, HID, Wiper and Rear window defogger. — Ground Distribution diagrams, image VA586554
    • VERIFIED (same Power Distribution 1 of 4): fuse 95 → C7-13 → PNK/YEL → S2442 ("right front door sill, w/ blind spot").
    • Branch XY4000A-12 → I701-J → S04A F923 → right and left rear blind spot sensors (pin 5).
    • Branch XY2120A-6 → I6742A-1 → RED → rear video camera pin 3.
    • Source: VA586498; local crop renders/2018_Durango_PD1of4_crop_RUN1_relay_F95_BSM_camera.png
    • VERIFIED (A/C diagram 1 of 4, VA585297):
    • Fuse 103 and Fuse 101 are both labelled "HOT W/ RUN 2 RELAY ENERGIZED".
    • Fuse 103 → C6-28 → C7-8 → REAR BLOWER MOTOR RELAY coil 85. Its coil 86 → C7-4 → S2505 → G919A.
    • The (front) BLOWER MOTOR RELAY coil 86 → C7-16 → S2505 → G919A.
    • Front blower relay contact 87 → FUSE 13 40A → C7-21 → DK BLU (to the blower power module, continued off-page).
    • Source: Air Conditioning diagrams, image VA585297
    • UNVERIFIED (low-resolution table in the PDC layout graphic): relay list reads approximately K08 "Run / Start", K12 "Run 2", K13 "Blower Motor - Rear", K15 "Run 1", K16 "Blower Motor". — PDC layout image G00621551
    • STRONG INFERENCE: the 2015 SRS diagram shows the same arrangement: "HOT W/ RUN 2" fuse 85 10A → C6-15 → ORC 104 PNK/YEL F929, and fuse 86 → C6-13 → ORC 103 PNK/LT GRN F945. — 2015 R/T AWD Supplemental Restraints
    • SECONDARY: an owner's-manual-derived fuse list gives F85 and F86 both "Airbag Module" 10A and F95 "Rear Camera / ParkSense" 10A. The same list gives F100 as 15A and F103 as 20A, which conflicts with the factory graphic and diagram (10A). — startmycar 2018 Durango fuse box

    Inferences¶

    • STRONG INFERENCE about the measurement. F85 reads 0 V on both test points in RUN while F86 and F95 are normal. So the fault is upstream of the fuse, i.e. the Run 2 relay output bus. An open F85 element would still leave 12 V on the supply-side test point.
    • STRONG INFERENCE: the supply chain that should energise Run 2 appears healthy:
    • Coil feed (Run 1 output bus) is live, since F95 is live.
    • Coil return is shared with the Run 1 coil (C1-12 → G909A), and Run 1 works.
    • Contact 30 is on the PDC B+ bus.
    • Prime suspects are therefore the Run 2 relay itself, its socket terminals, or an internal PDC bus or trace break.
    • Quick non-SRS cross-check: all fuses on the Run 2 output (F40, F103, F101, F100) should also read 0 V in RUN. Seeing 0 V on them confirms a dead Run 2 output bus without touching any ORC/SRS connector.
    • Consequence to confirm with other researchers: F103 (Run 2) feeds the rear blower motor relay coil, so the rear blower should also be dead.
    • Out of scope, but flagged for the synthesis writer: per the 2018 diagrams, BSM sensors and the rear camera are on F95 (Run 1), not F85. The front blower relay coil is on the Run 1 bus. Their failure therefore is not explained by a loss of F85 itself. It must come from something else, e.g. a different feed, a CAN/module dependency, or a shared ground such as S2505/G919A for the blower relay coils. Not investigated here.

    Gaps¶

    • The internal PDC bus structure cannot be serviced or probed from the diagrams; they only show logical connections. Run 2 relay socket cavity numbering was not found.
    • The front blower motor power module feed and ground (A/C diagram 2 of 4) were not reviewed. The renders saved are pages 1 of 4 only.

    Q3. ORC connectors: connector numbers, pins for Ignition RUN (F929) and RUN/START (F945), grounds, CAN, colours; in-line connectors or splices between PDC and ORC¶

    Takeaway¶

    VERIFIED. The ORC has three harness connectors. C1 = D3834A (IP harness, squibs only), C2 = D3834B (Body harness: power, ground, CAN and all sensors) and C3 = D3834C (side impact sensor 2). Power and ground are on C2: - pin 104 = F929 PK/YE 0.50 "FUSED IGNITION SWITCH OUTPUT (RUN)" (from F85) - pin 103 = F945 PK/GN 0.50 "IGNITION RUN/START CONTROL OUTPUT" (from F86) - pin 101 = Z913 BK 0.50 GROUND → G913A (under rear of center console) - CAN C on pins 53 (D12 YE, −) and 54 (D11 YE/DB, +)

    No in-line connector or splice is drawn on F929 or F945 between PDC C6 and ORC C2.

    Cited Findings¶

    • VERIFIED: ORC C2 (D3834B) is YELLOW, FEMALE, Body harness, PN F577100. Pins: 53 D12 YE 0.35 CAN C (−); 54 D11 YE/DB 0.35 CAN C (+); 101 Z913 BK 0.50 GROUND; 103 F945 PK/GN 0.50 IGNITION RUN/START CONTROL OUTPUT; 104 F929 PK/YE 0.50 FUSED IGNITION SWITCH OUTPUT (RUN). The remaining pins are sensors, squibs and tensioners: 55–99 including R41 at 99 "AIRBAG DEPLOYMENT SIGNAL". — 2018 ORC C2 end view
    • VERIFIED: ORC C1 (D3834A) is YELLOW, FEMALE, IP harness, PN F067100. Its pins are only front squib lines: 6/7 driver knee blocker; 11/12 driver squib 2; 13/14 passenger squib 2; 16/17 passenger squib 1; 18/19 driver squib 1. No power or ground. — 2018 ORC C1 end view
    • VERIFIED: ORC C3 (D3834C) is YELLOW, FEMALE, Body harness, PN F477100. Pins 1–4: right/left side impact sensor 2 signal and ground (R17–R20). — 2018 ORC C3 end view
    • VERIFIED (SRS diagram 1 of 3): the ORC is labelled "(UNDER REAR OF CENTER CONSOLE)" with connector label C2 on pins 53–104.
    • 104 PNK/YEL F929 runs to PDC C6-15.
    • 103 PNK/GRN F945 runs to PDC C6-13.
    • 101 BLK Z913 → ground G913A (UNDER REAR OF CENTER CONSOLE).
    • 53/54 D12/D11 → "COMPUTER DATA LINES SYSTEM".
    • No in-line connector symbol appears on the F929 or F945 lines. The only in-line connector in that area is XY5010A-28, on the R41 airbag deployment signal to the radio.
    • Source: VA582314
    • VERIFIED (Ground Distribution 3 of 4, VA586556): "OCCUPANT RESTRAINT CONTROLLER MODULE 101 C2 → BLK → G913A (UNDER REAR OF CENTER CONSOLE)". This is a dedicated eyelet, not shared with other modules on that page. — VA586556
    • VERIFIED (R/T AWD cross-check): the 2018 R/T AWD ORC C2 pin table is identical. — 2018 R/T AWD ORC C2
    • VERIFIED: ORC Operation text says the ORC "receives battery current through two circuits": a fused ignition output (run) circuit and a fused ignition output (run-start) circuit, each through a PDC fuse. It also says ground comes through "a take out of the instrument panel wire harness" secured by a ground screw. That text conflicts with the end-view table, where Z913 is on C2, the Body-harness-family connector. — ORC Operation
    • VERIFIED: the ORC is mounted on the floor-panel transmission tunnel under the center console. The IP and body harness connectors attach on the rearward-facing side with lever locks; disconnect the smaller connector first. — ORC Removal
    • STRONG INFERENCE (2015/2017): ORC C2 pins 101/103/104 are unchanged. 2015 uses Z913 BK, F945 PK/LG 22 ga and F929 PK/YL 22 ga. — 2015 R/T AWD ORC C2

    Inferences¶

    • STRONG INFERENCE: the ORC has no constant-B+ input per the 2018 diagram and pin tables. Only the two ignition feeds (C2-104 via F85/Run 2 and C2-103 via F86/Run-Start) plus ground C2-101 are present. The B212C/D "Theory of Operation" text says "battery and ignition feed", which looks like generic template text.
    • STRONG INFERENCE: PDC C6 and ORC C2 are both "Body" harness family, and no in-line connector is drawn. F929 is therefore most likely one continuous wire in the body harness from the PDC (right side of the engine compartment) to the center-console tunnel. An open in this wire is unlikely to explain the observation, because F85 is dead on its supply side.

    Gaps¶

    • Exact physical cavity positions of pins 101/103/104 on the D3834B face were not transcribed. The figure is saved as images/2018_Durango_ORC_C2_D3834B_end_view__GCWD1006287.png.
    • Harness routing and pass-through between the engine compartment and the cabin for F929 was not found in the pages reviewed.

    Q4. Factory diagnostic procedures for B212D-13 (Ignition RUN Only Input – Circuit Open) and B212C-13 (Ignition RUN/START Input – Circuit Open)¶

    Takeaway¶

    VERIFIED, with caveats. Both DTCs set when ignition voltage on the respective input is missing or below 6 V for more than 15 s while the battery circuit maintains system voltage. Both name the correct 2018 fuses in "Possible Causes": F85 for B212D (F929) and F86 for B212C (F945). However, the "Diagnostic Test" step text is templated from another platform: - it references "fuse F6" (B212D) and "fuse F24" (B212C), - it says "ORC module C1 body harness connector", - it says "Rear Power Distribution Center".

    Use 2018 mapping instead: F85/F86, PDC C6-15/13, ORC C2-104/103.

    Cited Findings¶

    • VERIFIED — B212D-13 Set Condition: "sets when the ORC fails to detect ignition voltage or if there is less than 6 volts on the ignition voltage supply circuit for more than 15 seconds while the battery circuit maintains system operating voltage". It transitions to stored after 5 s of normal voltage. — B212D-13 Set Condition
    • VERIFIED — When monitored (both DTCs): at power-up initialisation and every 100 ms during the ignition cycle. — B212D-13 When Monitored
    • VERIFIED — B212D-13 Possible Causes: Active PDC trouble codes; (F929) RUN RELAY CIRCUIT OPEN; POWER DISTRIBUTION CENTER FUSE F85 OPEN; (F929) FUSED RUN RELAY OUTPUT CIRCUIT OPEN OR SHORTED TO GROUND; ORC. The page also says to perform Pre-Diagnostic Troubleshooting first. — B212D-13 Possible Causes
    • VERIFIED — B212D-13 Diagnostic Test steps (paraphrased; full text saved):
    • Notes: use only a DMM that applies no more than 20 mA to a circuit. Probe ORC harness cavities only with Miller test lead 10159-6 (kit 10159) or equivalent. Battery must be fully charged.
    • (1) Ignition on; scan for active B212D-13. If it is not active, run the Intermittent Test.
    • (2) Ignition off, battery disconnected, wait 2 min; remove and inspect the "Airbag Run Circuit Fuse No. F6" [2018 = F85]. If open, go to 3; if OK, go to 6.
    • (3) Measure resistance F929 from the fuse terminal to ground; threshold 100 Ω.
    • (4) Disconnect the ORC connectors; measure F929 resistance at the "ORC module C1 body harness connector" [2018 = C2-104] to ground; threshold 100 Ω.
    • (5) Ignition on; measure voltage at the fuse supply side. Above 6.0 V → go to 6; otherwise "Repair the open (F929) Run Relay Output circuit".
    • (6) Reinstall the fuse, disconnect the ORC, follow the ignition-on/battery-reconnect warning. Measure F929 voltage at the ORC harness connector to ground. Above 6.0 V → replace ORC; otherwise repair the open F929 circuit.
    • Finally run the Restraints System Verification Test.
    • Source: B212D-13 Diagnostic Test
    • VERIFIED — B212C-13 Possible Causes: Active PDC trouble codes; (F945) RUN/START RELAY CIRCUIT OPEN; POWER DISTRIBUTION CENTER FUSE F86 OPEN; (F945) FUSED RUN/START RELAY OUTPUT CIRCUIT OPEN OR SHORTED TO GROUND; ORC. — B212C-13 Possible Causes
    • VERIFIED — B212C-13 Diagnostic Test steps (paraphrased):
    • (1) Read active DTC.
    • (2) Inspect "fuse F24" [2018 = F86].
    • (3) Resistance F945 from fuse output side to ground below 100 Ω?
    • (4) With both ORC connectors disconnected, F945 resistance at the "ORC module C1 body harness connector" [2018 = C2-103] to ground.
    • (5) Ignition on; voltage at the fuse supply side above 6.0 V?
    • (6) Fuse reinstalled, ORC disconnected, ignition on per warning; voltage at the ORC harness connector above 6.0 V?
    • (7) Reconnect; if B212C-13 is still active, replace the ORC.
    • Then run the Verification Test.
    • Source: B212C-13 Diagnostic Test
    • VERIFIED — The R/T AWD copies of both Diagnostic Test pages are identical, including the F6/F24 references. — R/T B212D-13 Diagnostic Test
    • VERIFIED — The B212D-16 (Voltage Below Threshold) Set Condition text says "less than 16 volts", which looks like a typo for 6 V. Its Possible Causes list "ACTIVE POWER DISTRIBUTION CENTER RUN RELAY TROUBLE CODES" and F85 open. — B212D-16 Set Condition
    • VERIFIED — Verification Test:
    • Reconnect everything except the battery, then follow the ignition-on warning.
    • Erase stored DTCs in all airbag modules, perform module reset, wait 1 min and read active then stored codes.
    • If the battery was disconnected, perform the Battery Reconnection procedure.
    • If the ORC was replaced, perform ORC initialisation, and ESC re-initialisation may be needed.
    • Source: Restraints System Verification Test

    Inferences¶

    • STRONG INFERENCE: the observed state (F85 0 V on both test points, F86 normal) should produce an active B212D-13 and no B212C. In the test flow, step 5 ("voltage at fuse supply side > 6 V?") would answer No, pointing to "open (F929) Run Relay Output circuit". On this platform the supply side of F85 is the PDC-internal Run 2 relay output bus, not a harness wire, so the actual repair target is the Run 2 relay or the PDC (see Q2).
    • UNVERIFIED (logic reading of the published text): B212D-13 step 4 says "below 100 Ω → Go To 5; not below → replace ORC". That looks inverted or garbled relative to the B212D-16 version, where "below 100 Ω → repair short to ground". Any low-resistance-to-ground finding on F929 with the ORC unplugged should be treated as a harness short. The page text should not be followed literally there.
    • No B212D test step needs probing at the ORC connector until the PDC side is restored. Fuse-cavity voltage checks at the PDC are non-SRS measurements. Checking F40/F103/F101/F100 confirms the bus without touching the SRS.

    Q5. ORC power and grounds diagram, and SRS service precautions¶

    Takeaway¶

    VERIFIED. The 2018 SI has no standalone "ORC Power & Grounds" diagram. Power and ground appear on Supplemental Restraints diagram 1 of 3 (VA582314) and in Ground Distribution 3 of 4 (G913A). Mandatory precautions: - Disconnect and isolate the negative battery cable; the IBS connector comes off first. - Wait 2 minutes for the ORC capacitor to discharge. - Use a DMM that applies no more than 20 mA, and only test lead 10159-6 at ORC connectors. - Never drop the ORC; a dropped unit must be replaced. - Repair SRS wiring only with Mopar kits. - Squib circuits on ORC C1 and C2 R-circuits must never be probed or powered.

    Cited Findings¶

    • VERIFIED: SRS Warning page:
    • Disable the SRS before any ORC diagnosis or service: disconnect and isolate the battery negative cable, then wait two minutes for the system capacitor to discharge. This is "the only sure way to disable the SRS".
    • An ORC with a rollover sensor rolled while powered will deploy the side curtains.
    • Never repair SRS circuits for which there is no Mopar wiring repair kit.
    • For welding, disconnect both battery cables and ground the positive cable to discharge the ORC capacitor.
    • Source: Restraints System Warning
    • VERIFIED: ORC Removal: disconnect the IBS connector first, then the negative cable, and wait two minutes. Never strike or drop the ORC; a dropped ORC "must be scrapped and replaced". — ORC Removal
    • VERIFIED: B212C and B212D tests both state the 20 mA DMM limit and test lead 10159-6. They give two ignition-on warnings:
    • Key ignition: turn the ignition on, then reconnect the battery and wait 2 min.
    • Keyless ignition: disconnect the battery and wait 2 min; remove the ORC fuses; reconnect the battery and wait 2 min; cycle ignition On; reinstall the ORC fuses; wait 2 min.
    • Source: B212C-13 Diagnostic Test
    • VERIFIED: the ORC contains an energy-storage capacitor that can deploy SRS components for up to one second after a battery disconnect or failure. — ORC Operation
    • VERIFIED: the ORC houses a microcontroller, electronic impact sensor, safing sensor, rollover sensor and energy-storage capacitor. The label arrow must point forward. — ORC Description
    • VERIFIED: SRS Diagnosis & Testing: hard-wired SRS circuits may be diagnosed conventionally, but SRS diagnosis requires a scan tool and may need the SRS Load Tool with jumpers and adapters. — SRS Diagnosis and Testing

    Inferences¶

    • Practical safe-diagnosis order consistent with the SI:
    • Diagnose F85's dead supply entirely at the PDC: fuse cavities, Run 2 relay, and the other Run 2 fuses F40/F103/F101/F100.
    • Only after F85 is live, check the C6-15 → ORC C2-104 continuity or voltage per B212D steps. Follow the battery-disconnect, 2-minute and test-lead rules.
    • Never back-probe the ORC C1 squib pins or the C2 R-circuits.
